`rotifer.genome.data.NeighborhoodDF.neighbors` raises `ValueError` for `strand='+'`, `strand='-'`, `fttype='any'` and target rules that fail to evaluate. Before, the strand values were ignored, `fttype='any'` returned an empty table and a broken rule was skipped with a log message. Use `strand=None` or `'same'`, `fttype='same'`, and fix the rule.
