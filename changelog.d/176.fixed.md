`rotifer.genome.io.parse` accepts any callable as the `assembly` rule (a `functools.partial` used to give `Assembly:Assembly:...`), reads text streams such as `io.StringIO`, and closes the files it opens. `rotifer.genome.io.gff` reads GFF files without a `##FASTA` section and looks up `organism` taxids with `rotifer.db.ncbi.TaxonomyCursor`; it used to fail on both.
